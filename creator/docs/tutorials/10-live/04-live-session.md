## Steps

1. Start the editor joined to the live session’s relay (--relay and the session’s address). Without it the sheet says “no session”.
2. In “Perform” open the “Session” sheet: its head shows the session code.
3. Choose where readers start: “Toon”, “Chapter” and a “Label” (empty = the chapter’s start).
4. Readers join with the QR code or the link: “Copy link” to send it, “Open here” to try it yourself.
5. The sheet lists the linked readers and the last story event it heard.
6. In “Show” the “Who” sheet lists everyone here: “move to …” gives a deck or a phone its role, and “ROLES” → “new role” adds one.
