## Steps

1. The red “PANIC” pill sits in the top bar of every workspace; Shift+Esc does the same from anywhere.
2. A panic is black and silence on every deck below this conductor and on the editor’s own stage. The log says “PANIC – every deck below black and silent”.
3. While it holds, the pill reads “RESUME”. Rows still fire in the show, but nothing reaches a stage.
4. Press “RESUME” to bring every deck back – to the cells the show holds now, not the ones that played before.

## Note

Shift+Esc only panics; only the pill resumes. Plain Esc at the top level quits the editor.
