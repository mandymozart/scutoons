## Steps

1. Open the show in “Show” and press “Flash” in the “Inspector”.
2. Choose the decks: “all”, “none”, “not on this version”, “ready”, a kind of device or “seat n”. Or type a filter, like where device:browser.
3. Choose “A suggestion” (each deck has 10 s to say no) or “Forced”, and whether the decks are “Locked”.
4. Press “Flash n …”, read the question, then “Flash” (or “Force flash”).
5. A deck shows “Flash · suggestion” with “Accept” and “Reject”. After a forced flash it keeps its own version, so it can go “Back to my version”.

## Note

Decks that join later get the flash too. A deck can ask “Please don’t flash”.
