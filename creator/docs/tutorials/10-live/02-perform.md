## Steps

1. Switch to “Perform”: “Cues” is the show’s matrix, “Next” what comes, “Stage now” what each outlet plays, the “Log” what happened.
2. A row’s ▶ fires the whole row; a cell’s ▶ launches only that cell. The stop at the head of a column stops that column.
3. “Next” shows what comes: “GO” plays it, “Drop” skips a suggestion.
4. Watch “Stage now” and the “Log” (newest first) to see what the decks really play.
5. Something wrong? “PANIC” (or Shift+Esc) is black and silence on every deck; “RESUME” brings back what the show holds now.

## Note

Esc alone leaves the editor – panic is Shift+Esc. Only the “RESUME” button resumes.
