## Steps

1. Select a slice and open the “Slice” tab in the inspector.
2. Fill the “Image” slot. For a video, fill the “Video” slot too – the image becomes its poster.
3. For a video choose “Loop”, “Autoplay” and “Controls”.
4. Choose the “Placement” (“In the reading column” or “Full viewport”), the “Aspect ratio” and how it should “Fit” – the previews show a phone and a desktop.
5. Tick “Sensitive content” if needed: readers who hide sensitive content see it blurred and muted.

## Note

A poster with another aspect ratio than its video makes the slice shift while it loads – the inspector warns about it.
