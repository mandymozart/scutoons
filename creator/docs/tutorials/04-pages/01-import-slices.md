## Facts

- Size: A slice is 390 px wide at 1x; upload 1170 px wide (3x) and the 2x and 1x are made from it.

## Steps

1. In the chapter tree select a group (or “Add group” at the bottom for a new one): its slices show as a strip.
2. “Import slices” under the strip lets you choose several images at once (JPEG, PNG or WebP).
3. “Import n images” shows each with its target: “New slice” or “Replace n · name”. A file named like slice-26 or 26 goes to slice 26.
4. Choose where new slices go (“after slice n”, “before slice n”, “at the end”), then press “Import · … replace, … new”.
5. In the group’s panel choose its “Layout”: “Vertical” (read top to bottom), “Horizontal” (side by side as you scroll) or “Endless” (a band readers drag).

## Note

A replaced slice keeps its texts, items, events and audio; its old picture stays as an earlier version. One image at a time: drop it on the middle of a slice to replace it, on its top or bottom edge to insert it.
