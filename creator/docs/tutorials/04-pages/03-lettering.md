## Steps

1. Select a slice, open the “Layers” tab in the inspector and press “+ Add text box”.
2. Pick a preset card – Narration, Speech, Thought, Onomatopoeia, Story, Scene or Lyrics. Or take the “Text box” tool in the canvas toolbar and drag on the slice.
3. Type the text. For a bubble choose a “Tail” (“Speech (wedge)” or “Thought (circles)”) and drag its tip and base on the slice.
4. “Advanced” holds the “Anchor”, “Font”, “Text size”, “Colour” (solid or a gradient) and the “Outline”.
5. To let it come in later, add an event in the “Events” tab that shows the text box and choose “How it comes in”: Appear, Pop, Slam, Type on and more.
6. Switch the text language in the top bar and type the translation. Position and size are the same in every language; only the text changes.
