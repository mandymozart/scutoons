## Steps

1. In a text field type “{”: the hint says “Enter = builder”. Press Enter for the builder, or keep typing the code.
2. In the builder choose “What”: a “Value”, “If … else”, “Per visit” or “Code”, then search the value – story variables and functions.
3. “Apply” puts it into the text as a chip. Click the chip to change it in the builder, double click to edit its code; Backspace removes it whole.
4. On the canvas “Preview texts with expressions” shows the text “as written”, for a “new guest”, “halfway” or with “everything collected”.

## Note

A brace as text needs a backslash before it. And = sets a value: to compare, write ==.
