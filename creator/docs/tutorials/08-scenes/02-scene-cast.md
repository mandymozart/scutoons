## Steps

1. Open a scene in Build: a double click on it in Show’s “Library”, or “open in Build” on a show cell.
2. The tree lists the scene and its members. Select one: “MEMBER” shows its holder, filler, spot and rig.
3. “Move” (W) drags a member on the stage, snapping; “Pick” (Q) only selects. “Grid & snap” sets the grid, the step and what it snaps to.
4. Under “VALUES IN THE SCENE” drag a knob to set it for this scene; the stage plays it when you let go and the scene saves itself. The “×” beside it goes back to its rig’s value.
5. “Open as rig” shows the member’s rig alone with the scene’s values on its knobs. Esc or the scene’s line brings them back into the scene.

## Note

“Rotate” (E) and “Scale” (R) switch the mode only; their handles are coming.
