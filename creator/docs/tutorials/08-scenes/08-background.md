## Steps

1. Select the rig (or the scene) in Build: the inspector shows a “background” row.
2. Click the swatch for the colour picker: the “PALETTE” (with green and blue screen), your “RECENT” colours, saturation and brightness, the hue.
3. The eyedropper takes the colour under the pointer on the stage; Esc cancels it.
4. Or click the value and type a colour (#rrggbb) or a picture’s media path.

## Note

A scene’s background wins over its rigs’, and a show’s over both. Empty means the modules’ (or the rigs’) background, else black.
