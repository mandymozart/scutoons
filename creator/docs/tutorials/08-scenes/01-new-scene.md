## Steps

1. In the “Show” workspace open the “Library” panel and press “new scene”.
2. Type an “id” – the scene’s address, scene:<id> – and a “name”. From the id on, the scene saves itself as a draft.
3. Below the cast, find your toon’s rigs and press one to add it to the cast (press again to take it off).
4. “Build” opens the scene on the stage: place the cast and set up its camera there.
5. Publish it in the creator: “Scenes”, filter by “Toon” and the “Draft” state, then “Publish”. The core checks first that a deck can play it.

## Note

Until it is published, a scene shows only in the editor that made it. The rigs come from the server, so sign the editor in first.
