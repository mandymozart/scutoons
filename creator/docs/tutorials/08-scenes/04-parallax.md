## Steps

1. Open a scene in Build and select the scene in the tree.
2. “Parallax stack” adds seven empty layers: a far matte, three full layers behind the 3D and strips left, right and below in front. “Add layer” adds one more, 4 m behind.
3. Each layer has a handle at its left edge on the stage. Drop a picture from the library on a handle to fill the layer.
4. Drag a handle up or down to move the layer in depth; the picture is fitted anew to the camera’s view there.
5. In the “PARALLAX LAYER” inspector “covers” sets full, left, right, top or bottom, and “Remove” takes the layer away.

## Note

A scene takes up to 12 layers.
