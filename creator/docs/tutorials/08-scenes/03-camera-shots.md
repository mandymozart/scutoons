## Steps

1. Open a scene in “Build” and open the “Camera tools” panel. Every section is one line saying what it is set to; click it to open it (Alt-click: all).
2. Under “LENS” choose “Perspective” or “Orthographic” and a focal length (17 to 150 mm; click the exact field for any). Under “HEIGHT” the figures frog, cat, kid, adult, cyclops, giraffe and bird set the eye height.
3. Frame the stage with the mouse, then press “+ from view” under “SHOTS”. Give the shot an aim: “its point” or a member of the cast.
4. Under “MOVE” pick a kind from its group – locomotion, lens or accents (glide, push in, orbit, zoom, cut …), the shot it “ends on” and its length in beats, bars or seconds. “Preview” plays it, “Keep as move” keeps it.
5. A “fly through” runs along the “TRACK”: “Add track”, then “Add point” for each stop.
6. Switch views in the tools pill: “Free view” (0), “Primary camera”, each shot (1–9), “Previous”, “Next”. In a show a cell picks a shot as scene:<id> > shot:<id>.
