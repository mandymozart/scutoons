## Steps

1. Open a scene in “Build” and open the “DOP” panel.
2. Under “LENS” choose “Perspective” or “Orthographic” and a focal length (17 to 150 mm, or “mm…” for any). The chips frog, cat, kid, adult, giant, giraffe and bird set the eye height.
3. Frame the stage with the mouse, then press “Add from view” under “SHOTS”. Give the shot an aim: “its point” or a member of the cast.
4. Under “MOVE” pick a kind (cut, glide, push in, orbit …), the shot it “ends on” and its length in beats, bars or seconds. “Preview” plays it, “Keep as move” keeps it.
5. A “fly” runs along the “TROLLEY TRACK”: “Add track”, then “Add point” for each stop.
6. Switch views in the tools pill: “Free view” (0), “Primary camera”, each shot (1–9), “Previous”, “Next”. In a show a cell picks a shot as scene:<id> > shot:<id>.
