## Facts

- Already signed in: Signed in on scutoons.com means signed in here too.

## Steps

1. Open creator.scutoons.com. Under “Creator sign-in” type your email and press “Send code”.
2. Type the “One-time code” from the mail and press “Sign in” – or just tap the link in the mail.
3. “No access” means your account has no creator role yet: ask your lead, or try another email with “Switch account”.
4. The start page shows “Continue” (where you were last), your “Toons”, what is “Not live yet” and what is “Waiting for you”.
5. Ctrl+K (or /) opens “Search or jump to …”: type a toon, a chapter or a page and press Enter. Alt+↑ and Alt+↓ go a level up or down.

## Note

Accounts are made in the reader on scutoons.com; the creator opens for accounts with the creator role.
