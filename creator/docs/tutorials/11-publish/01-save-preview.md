## Steps

1. “Save” in the bottom bar stores a draft; a dot on it means there are unsaved changes. Only creators see drafts.
2. “Add a note?” asks what changed – a suggestion is already typed, so just type over it, or press “Save without note”.
3. In the chapter editor the round phone button in the top bar opens the “Reader” in the dock; “Screen” switches between “Phone” and “Desktop”.
4. The reader opens at the group and slice you are on and follows you. It shows saved drafts only and reloads after every save.
5. Unsaved edits wait in this browser: after a reload a bar offers “Restore” or “Discard”.

## Note

In the chapter editor Ctrl+S saves at once, without the note; on the other pages it asks. In the deck editor everything saves itself.
