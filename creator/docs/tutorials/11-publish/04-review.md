## Facts

- Who reviews: Set in the admin per account and toon: “Reviews publishing requests” or “Submits for review”. Without a rule, an account publishes itself.

## Steps

1. When work waits for you, the start page shows “Waiting for you” with its count.
2. Each submission shows the toon, the files, who sent it, when, and their note. Click its name to open the page and look.
3. “Publish” makes it live as submitted; the author gets a mail.
4. Or “Send back” with a note to the author: they get it by mail, and the drafts stay as they are.

## Note

“Newer drafts exist – not part of this review” means the author changed more since: only the submitted version goes live.
