## Steps

1. “History” in the bottom bar holds “Undo”, “Redo” and “Versions”. Outside a text field Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes.
2. “Versions” lists every saved version: when, by whom, its note, and whether it is “current” or “in review”.
3. “Restore” brings an earlier version back as a new draft. Nothing is published until you publish it.
4. A published version has “Unpublish”: readers then see nothing until a version is published again.

## Note

Chapters and seasons hold every language in one version, so unpublishing takes them all. Accounts that submit for review see no Publish or Unpublish here.
