## Steps

1. “Problems” in the bottom bar shows its count; press it to open the list. On a phone it is the ⚠ button.
2. The head counts errors and warnings. Each line says where (group, slice, line, language) and what is wrong.
3. Click a line to jump to the place and fix it there.
4. A warning that is fine can be accepted with its ✓ (“Looks fine – accept the warning”). That is an edit like any other, so save it.
5. Errors can’t be accepted. Publishing with errors asks “Publish anyway?” first.

## Note

Tick “accepted” or “notes” to see the accepted warnings and the quiet notes again.
