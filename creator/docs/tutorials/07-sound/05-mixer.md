## Steps

1. Open the “Mixer” panel (bottom right): one strip per track at the level shown, plus the “out” strip.
2. The pills on top choose the level: “rig” for the tracks, or a track you entered for the gains inside it.
3. Drag a fader – it goes into the draft when you let go. Double click the dB value to type one, Enter sets it, Esc leaves it.
4. Drag the pan bar for left and right; “M” mutes the track.
5. The peak readout holds the loudest moment, red once it went over 0 dB. Click it to clear it.

## Note

A fader fed by a cable has no handle: the cable sets it.
