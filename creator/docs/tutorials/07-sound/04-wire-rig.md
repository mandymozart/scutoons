## Steps

1. Build starts in the wiring: every track is one body with its outlets. Double click a body (or point at it and press Tab) to go in.
2. Drag from an out to an in to connect them. Pull a cable off an in and let go to cut it, or drop it on another in to move it.
3. Right click a port (or the stage) and “type to find”: it lists the ports that one can reach.
4. “Add” beside the rig picker adds a node inside a track, a “world · far” node with its holder from the address book, or at the rig a track playing an instrument or a module.
5. “Remove” (or Delete) takes the selected node or track away with its cables; Ctrl+Z takes any change back.
6. The crumbs on top lead back: “rig” shows every track as one body again. Esc or Shift+Tab goes one level up.

## Note

At the rig level Esc quits the editor. Audio can’t go into a control in.
