## Steps

1. On the stage a module shows one ring cluster per area, like “SRC · 4”. A click opens it in place, another click closes it.
2. Select the node itself (not its track): the “Inspector” shows “NODE”, then “PARAMETERS” with a row of area pills.
3. Press an area – SRC, FLTR, AMP, LFO, VOICE on the analog synth – to open its first page. Press it again for its next page; it wraps round.
4. Each page is two rows of four knobs: drag or use the wheel to turn one in the engine. Twin pages put the same idea on the same knob, like the level of OSC 1 and OSC 2.
5. Parameters that are on no page follow below as bars, then the “PORTS”.

## Note

A parameter fed by a cable shows “< source” and can’t be turned by hand. To open a page straight away: --select NODE --page "SRC/OSC 2".
