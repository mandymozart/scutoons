## Steps

1. Open the Kanon from the toon’s “Script” tab, a place’s “Kanon” link on the map, or the “Kanon” square of a character, persona or look.
2. An empty one offers “Start empty”, “Start with the headings” or – in a translation – a copy of the source.
3. Write in sections: “## heading” opens one. Sections are optional and their order is fixed – the rules are in the “Info” panel.
4. Save drafts as you go. Publishing makes it Kanon: the generators, the artist briefs and the AI connection read the published text.

## Note

The Kanon is the team’s story text: readers never see it.
