## Steps

1. Open your toon from “Toons” (or with Ctrl+K): the last row of its chapter list is “+ New chapter”.
2. “+ New chapter” takes the next free number, adds the chapter to its season as a draft and opens it. Readers see nothing yet.
3. In the tree on the left choose “Meta” and fill in “Title”, “Description”, “Release” and “Author” (the author is required).
4. Drop pictures on the “Thumbnail” and “Key visual” slots, and add who is “Featuring” – they must be in the season’s cast.
5. “Status” is set per language: “published”, “draft” or “coming soon” (listed, not readable yet). It is kept in the season.
6. “Save” keeps it all as a draft and asks for a short note. Ctrl+S saves at once, without asking.

## Note

The chapter number is its address and never changes. If “+ New chapter” is grey, save the toon’s language list first.
