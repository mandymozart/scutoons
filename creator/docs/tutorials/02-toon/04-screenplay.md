## Steps

1. Open your toon’s “Script” tab: the Kanon, one screenplay per season and one per chapter, each “published”, “draft” or “not written”.
2. Open one: it is read-only at first – “Edit script” unlocks it.
3. Write in the screenplay format: the spaces count – action at 4, the character’s name in capitals at 25–30, dialogue at 16. Tab puts in 4 spaces.
4. The “Outline” on the side lists the scenes; “Format rules” opens all the rules.
5. Save and publish as everywhere else. A translation may be partial: the source language is the master.

## Note

The screenplay is for the team – readers never see it.
