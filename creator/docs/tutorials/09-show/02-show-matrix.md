## Steps

1. In “Show” the “Rows” panel is the show: “new row” adds a moment, each column is an outlet (a deck or a screen).
2. Drag a published scene from the “Library” onto a cell. A double click opens the scene in Build instead.
3. Select the cell: “piece” names its scene and “shot” its camera shot. Under “VALUES” set the cell’s knobs; “×” clears one.
4. “open in Build” puts the cell’s scene on the stage. Turn the knobs there, then “values → cell” writes them back and “back to Show” returns.
5. The show saves itself (Ctrl+S at once). The “Problems” sheet shows what a deck would not play.

## Note

“Load” in the inspector opens another show.
