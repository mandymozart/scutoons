## Steps

1. In “Show” open the “Sheet” panel and its “Demos” tab.
2. A press opens a demo in place of the show. Its first row says what to try.
3. “far-nodes” shows far nodes, “new-scene” making a scene, “values-and-scenes” scenes on cells, “vote-and-question” a vote.
4. Follow the first row’s “try:” steps, then open the next demo.

## Note

A demo replaces the open show – save yours first. From the command line: --demo NAME.
