## Steps

1. In “Show” switch the tools to “2D” and open the “Nodes” panel.
2. It draws the row: what comes from far, the cells and what the row sends, with typed pins and wires.
3. “LR” or “TB” lays it out left to right or top to bottom; the editor remembers your choice.
4. The wheel zooms, a middle drag (or Space + drag) pans, “Fit” or F shows it all.

## Note

The Nodes panel hides while a sheet is open.
