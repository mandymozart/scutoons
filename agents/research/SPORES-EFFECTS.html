Spores effects

Eight sketches of what the picture can do when many decks share one world and one clock. Concepts to try on live setups – none is built. Each one says what travels over the network and what stays on the deck.

clock control (values) audio picture scene over the network

1Shared world, local view

Decks exchange state, a seed and the clock – never pixels. Each deck draws the same moment as richly as its hardware allows.

  • Rule: an effect of a higher tier adds to the picture; it never changes what happens. Position, timing and colour of the main things are the same on all three.
  • Try: one scene on a Pi and a PC side by side, started from the same seed – film both screens.

2One canvas across decks

Every deck is a window into the same space. Put the screens side by side and a thing walks from one deck to the next.

  • Each deck knows: where its screen stands in the room (the stage environment) – its window follows from that, gaps included.
  • Travels: clock and state. A deck that joins late draws the right slice at once.
  • Try: three screens of different size in a row, one firefly swarm crossing them.

3One scene, many outlets

The core describes the frame once. A screen, a laser, the DMX lights and the phones each read the part they can show.

  • Toon edges and the laser share a source, not a pass: on a screen the edges come from the depth / normal / id prepass; the laser needs the same edges as lines, so the core extracts them once as geometry.
  • Try: the character's outline on the laser while the screen shows the filled toon picture of the same frame.

4Phones as a swarm – the Rotor experiment (#440)

A hundred phones in a dark room are a hundred points of one picture and a hundred voices of one synth. Each phone runs the browser deck and knows only its own place and the clock.

  • Decided for Rotor (2026-11-08): about 60 phones, over the internet, places arbitrary ("sit in a circle"), late is fine. Triggers and parameters – no audio stream.
  • The phone is the lowest render tier: the same core in wasm, a fullscreen shader with a handful of uniforms. Nothing phone-only to write.
  • Try first: a wave with a known speed, so lateness shows as a wobbling front and can be measured from a video.

5Sound into picture – FFT and envelopes as uniforms

The audio engine already measures the sound. The same numbers reach every shader on the deck as uniforms, and every other deck and phone as control values.

  • Built today: the engine's FFT, follower / env modules, the shader node's four knobs a–d. First wire-up: #746.
  • New: a fixed block with names, so a shader written for one scene reads the sound in every scene without wiring.
  • Try: the toon outline's width on the kick envelope; the phones' colour on the bands.

6Shaders in the Library

A shader is a document like a rig or a preset: saved once, checked by the core, named by any scene. Today a scene's shader node points at a WGSL file beside it.

  • Already decided (2026-10-30): visuals are data – WGSL and scenes reload; a module may hand over WGSL. OSCT plugins already carry shader files.
  • The step: a shader: kind in the Library with its knobs declared, the same way module presets become documents (#808).
  • Decided 2026-11-25: WGSL is the Library's shader language; a showpiece shader is shared like any other and carries a flag for the tier it needs (RENDER-TIERS.md §6).
  • Open: one language for deck and XR, or OSCT's shaders stay their own kind.

7The clock is read, never waited on

Audio runs in steady blocks. Each deck draws as fast as it can and asks the clock what time its next picture will be seen. A stronger GPU means more pictures, not a different time.

  • What can still disturb the sound: the processor, not the GPU – compiling a shader or uploading a big texture in the middle of a show. Do both while the scene loads.
  • What limits an RTX showpiece: the GPU layer (wgpu) does not hand out every RTX feature; ray queries, ray tracing pipelines and mesh shaders are there but experimental (30.0.1), DLSS is outside it. A showpiece build can reach the raw Vulkan device for one effect.

8All of it in one room

One evening's setup with everything above: a strong deck on the wall, two small decks widening the canvas, a laser tracing the outline, the wash on the kick, the audience as a ring of light.

  • What carries the evening: shaders from the Library (6), all reading the same sound block (5).
  • Smallest version to test: one PC, one Pi, ten phones – the wave of sketch 4 running through the screens of sketch 2.
