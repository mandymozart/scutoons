Everything on the page today, grouped into seven areas and played through three ways. Switch the reader's state, where the page was opened from, and the language. Recommendation

C: the page as A, plus a quick panel in the chapter

The page stays one view with no new routes, and the groups with summary lines give it order. In a chapter, the reader reaches volume, vibration, hints, sensitive content, quality and “save this chapter” without leaving the spot. B is the familiar app pattern but adds a level of back navigation from the chapter and makes pages with two settings.

Accessibility

Same rules in all three

  • One h1; each group an h2 holding a real <button aria-expanded aria-controls>, with the summary in its name.
  • B: the index is a nav; closing a sub-page puts focus back on its row. C: the panel is a dialog, Escape and pull-down close it, focus returns to the menu.
  • Every row and toggle at --control-height (44 px on touch); segmented choices are radio groups in a fieldset.
  • Saves announce “Gespeichert” (role=status), errors role=alert.
  • Reduced motion: no chevron or panel animation. Sizes in rem; round only for standalone icon buttons.
