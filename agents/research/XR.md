## 0 · What the refinement of 2026-11-20 changed

1. **OSCT's model is now the one SCUTOONS asked for.** An experience is an ordered list of scenes; a scene has free surfaces, each with a size in mm and a place; targets lie on a surface; an entry names its scene. Pages, spreads and formats are gone, the book is optional data (§2). The one real conflict of 2026-11-10, surfaces without pages, is gone, and its workaround ("a number stands in for the page") must not be built.
2. **Shape A stands; what it keeps has changed.** The tree is experience › scenes › surfaces › targets, with entries beside (§4), not surface › entry › target › scene.
3. **The word "scene" moved.** In OSCT a scene is now a step of the experience (a double page, a wall, a room) with its surfaces. What plays on a found target is the target's **entity**, a tree of nodes. The owner's "scene" of 2026-11-10 (§5.4) meant the second. And SCUTOONS's own word rule keeps `scene:` for the deck scene (SCENES.md §8). Proposal in §4.3; for the owner (§9.2).
4. **A type is a preset, not a stage.** Book, Concert reading, Exhibition and Poster say which scenes and surfaces a new experience starts with. The stage is one for all: it draws a scene from its surfaces. This replaces "the stage by type" and answers which surfaces each type takes: any (§8.1).
5. **A surface's place is content, not the editor's only.** OSCT stores it in the contract, and world tracking will place the room by it (osct#299, #682).
6. **What an entry does** has a second road: OSCT brings rules on the scene and the experience and a `goto` action whose address the host resolves, in SCUTOONS's address grammar (osct#334, decided there 2026-11-20). The two layers of §8.2 stay; a jump out of an experience can later be OSCT's own `goto`.
7. **A deck can hear XR.** What an entry does are story events, run by the reader's runner. A reader linked to a session sends them on as every story event, so a show's row or a deck scene's listener hears them (SCENE-EVENTS.md). No new mechanism (§8.2).
8. **Share or copy is open again.** OSCT's research recommends that SCUTOONS consumes the engine and the contract as packages at a pinned tag, and the editor as a kit behind one backend interface, in place of a copy (§6, §9.1). The owner's word so far is "copy".
9. **The editor's place and look are settled by the creator's redesign:** a workspace "Experiences" in the new shell, an experience a tab by its address, the Library and the panels shared (redesign/PLAN.md, owner 2026-11-20). §8.4's two questions fall away.
10. **Print is part of the method now:** targets take their place from the print PDF (osct#321), a PDF export makes books, posters and wallpaper with their targets (osct#169), and a target's recognition data can be made ahead (schema 4.1, 4.2).

## 4 · The model, and where it lands in SCUTOONS

### 4.1 The tree (shape A, the owner 2026-11-11, on schema 4.x)

```
experience:<id>                     SCUTOONS's document (catalog kind `experience`, versioned)
├─ SCUTOONS's fields                type · form (record) · toons · texts per language · per entry: its story events
└─ configuration                    OSCT's `GameConfiguration`, kept as it is
   ├─ experience                    id, title, book?, environment?, appearance?, passes?
   ├─ groups                        a label over several scenes (a chapter, a room, "Front matter")
   ├─ scenes, in order              a step: a double page, a cover, a wall, a room, a poster
   │  └─ surfaces                   name, size in mm, place in the scene
   │     └─ (targets lie on one)    by `target.place.surface`
   ├─ entries                       each names its scene (and surface); the reader's found thing
   │  └─ target                     the printed image, its place on a surface
   │     └─ entity                  what plays on it: a tree of nodes, with rules
   ├─ entities                      shared trees, named by `ref`
   └─ plugins, subtitles, credits
```

Stored flat and linked by ids, shown stacked in the editor (the owner's question of 2026-11-10, answered by OSCT's code then and now). The editor shows two stacks over the same rows: scene › surfaces › targets (where things lie) and scene › entries › target › nodes (what they are).

### 4.2 The types as presets

| Type | A new experience starts with | Later |
|---|---|---|
| **Book** | cover, spreads (one surface of twice the page's width each), back; `experience.book` filled | scenes added per double page; groups as chapters |
| Book as **record** | front, back, gatefold (one surface, or two side by side), the disc's label if wanted: all free surfaces | — |
| **Poster** | one scene with one surface in the poster's size | a poster wall: one scene with several surfaces in a row |
| **Exhibition** | one scene per room, its walls standing (rx 90), a table lying; a group per floor or part | plaques as targets on a wall's surface |
| **Concert reading** | one scene with a screen (a standing surface in the screen's size) and what lies on a table or is handed out | a scene per piece |

The type is SCUTOONS's field beside the configuration (shape A); OSCT may later take it as `experience.kind` (its D9). It changes nothing in the engine. A size to reuse ("LP sleeve 315 × 315", "A1 upright") is an entry of the "+ surface" menu, not a stored thing.

### 4.3 Words

| Word | In XR | Never |
|---|---|---|
| **experience** | the unit; `experience:<id>` | — |
| **scene** (of an experience) | OSCT's scene: one step with its surfaces. Always said with its experience and referenced below it, `experience:<id>.<scene>` | `scene:<id>`: that is the deck scene (SCENES.md §8.5) |

| **target** | the printed image the camera finds | — |

This is a proposal: OSCT's words are taken as they are (XR-SHARED-CORE.md §5: no renames inside), and the clash with the deck scene is kept apart by the address, as SCENES.md §8.2 #2 asks.

**In the address book (#477):** references, no addresses until a receiver exists ([address]): `experience:<id>` · `experience:<id>.<scene>` · `experience:<id>.<target>` (a target's id is unique in its experience; it defaults to its entry's id). ## 8 · Concepts (proposals: nothing here is decided until the owner says so)

### 8.1 Surfaces and the stage (draft 5, refined)

- A surface is OSCT's: a label, a size in mm, a place in its scene. Whether it stands or lies is its place (rx 90 stands). Draft 5's "format", "pages" and "room as a level" are out.
- **A room is a scene with several surfaces** (three or four walls, a table); several rooms are several scenes, under a group if they belong together. **A poster wall is one scene** with the posters as surfaces in a row. **A book is one scene per double page.** So the owner's question, whether a room is a thing of its own, is answered by the model: it is a scene, with a label.
- **One stage for all types:** it draws the open scene from its surfaces where they are placed; the Scenes filmstrip leafs through the scenes. "One room at a time" follows.
- The place is content: the reader's image tracking does not need it, the editor and later world tracking do.

### 8.2 What a target does (draft 6, refined)

- **Two layers, as before.** Inside the experience: OSCT's rules as they are, on a node today, on the scene and the experience with OSCT's coming MAJOR. Out of it, into SCUTOONS: an entry carries a list of SCUTOONS's story events (give and take item, set variable, unlock location, jump to a chapter, group, season, toon or product), stored in SCUTOONS's field beside the configuration, by the entry's id.
- **The engine only reports** (`targetUnlocked`, `targetTapped`: core events of the contract); the reader's page runs the entry's events with the runner the chapters use, so an item reaches the carrier bag as it does from a story.
- **Jumps:** out of the experience, a story event `jumpTo` today. Inside it, to another scene or target, OSCT's `goto` once it ships; its address grammar is SCUTOONS's, and the host resolves it, so `goto` can later name a chapter too and the entry's jump event becomes the fallback, not a second way.
- **A deck hears it** (new): the entry's events are story events with stable ids. A reader linked to a session (#522) sends them to the conductor as it does from a chapter; a show's row fires on them, a deck scene's listener can make a character join (SCENE-EVENTS.md §3). A concert reading's screen can so answer a found target in the hall. "Found" and "unlocked" themselves are not story events; an entry that should be heard carries one.
- **A story goes in and comes back:** a chapter's XR jump → the reader's XR page → found, the entity plays → unlocked, the events run → a jump back into the chapter, which can test the variable.
- Asked (unchanged): events on unlocked and on tapped, or on every find too; an entry that is only events and is not listed for the reader; 1 and 0 where a rule's true or false is a story variable.

### 8.3 The reader's XR mode (drafts 7 and 10)

- **Screens on a phone:** the page opens and names what to look for, with "Start the camera" and the way back; scanning (the count of found targets, the list); found (the entity on the picture, the reward as it enters the carrier bag); the entry as a sheet over the camera, with "Back to the story"; the camera refused (one message, "Try again", the way back).
- **The list** shows found targets only; the rest stays hidden (the owner). What it tells of the rest, a count or nothing, is open. Scenes are its sections (draft 10 grouped by surface: by scene now; a group is a heading above).
- **Whose is what:** the engine (camera, tracking, entities, the unlock effect) is OSCT's; everything drawn is Svelte, written new in the viewer; between them a small controller without a framework (OSCT plans the same as `reader-core`, after February 2027: SCUTOONS's comes first and can be its model).
- **What the page must give the engine** (OSCT's research §1.2): a transparent route (the camera canvas lies behind it), the tracking script from SCUTOONS's own origin with its licence, one configuration and one plugin registry per page load.
- **What loads when:** the first screen needs the experience's document only; "Start the camera" loads the engine and the tracking once; a found target loads its files in the quality level the device chooses. Readers who never open XR load none of it.
- Asked (unchanged): wait for "Start the camera", or straight into the camera when coming from a story; where what is found is kept; OSCT's tutorial, splash and sounds left out of the first step.

### 8.4 The editor in the creator (drafts 8 and 9, refined)

- **Where:** the workspace "Experiences" of the redesigned creator (redesign/PLAN.md §4: surfaces on a 3D tabletop; panels Tree, Inspector, Scenes filmstrip, Library, Tools, Reader simulated); an experience opens as a tab by its address (`experience:poster-feilchen`); "New…" offers an experience, poster first.
