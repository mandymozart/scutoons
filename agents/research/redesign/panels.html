Draft: panels, tabs, presets

  • Every section is a tab – Tree, Toon, Inspector, Flow, Preview, Problems, History, Library, Moods, Kanon … none is a dock.
  • Drag a tab by its pill into another panel's head, or out onto the stage → a panel of its own. A panel whose last tab leaves closes.
  • Panel head, right to left: × close · – fold · ◱ main (new: the front tab takes the stage) · ⤢ over everything (as OSCT's asset manager: a dialog over all, it takes priority).
  • A tab on the stage has its own head: ◱ again puts it back into its panel.
  • A workspace is a preset: which panels, their tabs, their places, which tab is main. Switch presets above – nothing else changes between them.
  • The stage is everything: the whole window, under the bar too – the bar is pills floating on it, like the panels. Open or folded, every panel floats the same; the stage never gives way.
  • Find sits in the bar's middle on Home; in an item it is the path. Language and Day / Night live in Settings only.

T = day / night. Not real data.
