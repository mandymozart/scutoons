always · the kit's dogma
Grounds are translucent and never grey.
Every ground – a control's, a panel's, a menu's, a popover's, a dialog's, a box's, a card's – is see-through, the stage showing through. An opaque grey fill does not exist. A grey ground in a draft or a capture is not the reference (your word, 2026-11-24).
The stage is black by night and white by day · a panel and a menu are the stage colour at one strength (0.85, one token) · a control is the text colour at 6 %, hover 10 %, selected 16 % · words on a see-through ground keep their ink ring.
