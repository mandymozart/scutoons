A · One Log, Problems as a filter

⠿ ⤢ ◱ – ×

One place, one set of pills. "Problems" is a filter of the Log: the default view shows what's wrong now on top (it stays until fixed), and below it what happened. One count, one list, one way to scope. Cost: a busy session puts many events under the problems; the Problems filter hides them.

B · Two tabs in one shared panel, the scope shared

⠿ ⤢ ◱ – ×

Problems and what happened stay apart. Each tab is a clean list, but the scope pills repeat on both tabs and must stay in sync: change the scope on Problems and the Log changes too, which reads as two lists pretending to be separate. Cost: two counts, two filters to learn, the "weird" shared pills.
