One Log or two tabs

A · One Log, Problems as a filter – chosen

⠿ ⤢ ◱ – ×

Event types with their legend: every event carries its type's icon; the Type pills show the same icons and pick which kinds show (several at once, none = all); the legend under the list says what each means. Problems aren't typed – they're the standing part. One place, one set of pills. "Problems" is a filter of the Log: the default view (All) shows the problems on top and the events below. One count, one list, one way to scope. Cost: a busy session puts many events under the problems; the Problems filter hides them.

B · Two tabs, the scope shared – considered, not chosen

⠿ ⤢ ◱ – ×

Problems and what happened stay apart. Each tab is a clean list, but the scope pills repeat on both tabs and must stay in sync: change the scope on Problems and the Log changes too, which reads as two lists pretending to be separate. Cost: two counts, two filters to learn, the "weird" shared pills.
