Packen: ein Knopf, ein Ziel

Drafts for agents/research/PACKING.md at phone width. The pack mode and the chest view are clickable: tap a lit container, a dimmed one, or an item in the open DJ trolley. Example inventory, not a real reader's.

Lit, dimmed, why

  • Lit: cyan glow plus an ink frame, with its fill (12 / 90). Tap: it goes in, a toast offers Rückgängig.
  • Dimmed + reason: Nur CDs · Nur Kassetten · Erst zu Hause aufstellen · Voll. Still focusable, so a screen reader says why.
  • No caption: not a container. Escape or ✕ leaves the mode and goes back to the item.
  • Desktop: dragging onto a container still works and shows the same lit and dimmed state.
