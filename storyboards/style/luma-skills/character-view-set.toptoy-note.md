# toptoy — Project Note

source: image:RlrWx2eH (official toptoy design render), captured 2026-09-30

Locked design facts for the **toptoy** character. Read this before generating any toptoy view; treat it as the identity authority alongside the reference render.

## The Pair

toptoy is a duo of two fuzzy, sugar-frosted 3D monsters. Their head and body colours are flipped opposites:

- **Yellow-head character** — yellow head, orange body. Blue horns tipped with pink and light blue, pink eyebrows, turquoise/blue nose, red lips, white teeth.
- **Orange-head character** — orange head, yellow body. Pink spiked crown; the upper-left horn has a through-hole; turquoise nose, red lips, white teeth. Raised skin-coloured bumps (two flanking the horns, four on the forehead) — same colour as the skin beneath, not separate features.

## Hands and Feet

- Each hand has exactly **3 basic rounded fingers**; each foot has exactly **3 basic rounded toes**. Never four.

## Gradient Markings

- Reproduce the soft **pink-to-body gradient shading** on the extremities and torso, never flattened:
  - Orange-bodied character: pink gradient on the **hands, stomach, and feet**.
  - Yellow character: pink gradient on the **hands and feet**.

## Style

- Fuzzy, candy / sugar-frosted 3D render; bright saturated colours; soft even lighting.

## Colour-Flip Rule

- Head and body are always opposite colours: yellow head → orange body; orange head → yellow body. Keep this flip intact in every view.
