# Storyboard taste – the owner's feedback, distilled

Read this with `storyboard.md` before drafting or changing a storyboard. Every rule carries the owner's words and the
date. New feedback: add it here (one rule, the quote, the date) **and** into the shot's `review` when it is about a
shot. Rules the checker can enforce go into `scripts/storyboard/validate.mjs` too. Repeated feedback = a missing rule.

## Look
- **Sin City.** Pure black and white, extreme contrast, at most one accent colour per scene – only where the story
  earns it. "dark black and white schema like sin city" (2026-10-09)
- **Extremely hard light, never diffuse.** One key light, hard cast shadows, no fill. "Extreme hard light. Not
  diffusion" (2026-10-09). Textures and detail matter little: "with the extreme light it will not be important to
  have textures and details" (2026-10-09).
- **Outlines only around the outside of a figure** – no lines at joints or inside a body – and **organic**, varying
  in width. "outlines better on the outside of figurines only. The joints look weird with outlines" · "make outlines
  vary in stroke width. So its a bit more organic" (2026-10-09)
- **Line art, not hand-drawn – with intense black and white so the light reads.** Clean, crisp contour lines on white,
  solid black shadow shapes from the one hard light; no hatching, no grey marker shading, no sketchy strokes. "the
  prompt was also not handdrawn, more line art, but keep intense bw to see lighting clearly" (2026-10-09, with storyboard
  references: line-art tigers, inked tigers, a film storyboard sheet)
- **The mountain tiger** (the owner's design 2026-10-09): white, muscular, half upright, big claws, the rune glyph on the
  forehead and on the body (glowing blue in colour; in black and white a solid black glyph). The figurine follows it as we
  refine. (media/characters/catpope-mountaintiger)
- **Images, not schematics.** "skip the svg and just go into images" (2026-10-09)
- **Detailed figurines** that match our characters, with a simple skeleton to pose; refine as we go. "more detailed
  figurines", "We can refine mannequins as we go" (2026-10-09)

## Story in the shots
- **Light is decided when the shots are written**, from the script – not later. "Important to also give light
  instructions during the script to shot descriptions phase" (2026-10-09)
- **Personas and perspective jump, and the storyboard must say so.** Every figure: persona + look; every shot: view
  (real / belief / narration / memory). "in the Forrest he is as human and the tiger. And if narrating sometimes as cat
  pope regular. So it jumps between these states and perspectives" (2026-10-09)
- **Catpope ch3: the tiger is PLF himself; he fights himself – and nature – but believes it is a tiger.** "He is
  battling themelf basically. But believe to be fighting a tiger." · "And nature." (2026-10-09)
- **Moods are the registry's moods** (acceptance, angry, bargaining, confused, crying, denial, disgusted, dizzy,
  embarrassed, furious, happy, laughing, neutral, sad, scared, smug, surprised, thinking) – the character images
  exist for those; nuance goes into the note. (from the reference packs, 2026-10-09)

## Formats and length
- **Safe formats are a restriction for what we suggest and prompt:** vertical ≤ 1:7 (plan ≤ 1:4), panoramas ≤ 13
  screens (plan ≤ 8; Luma's proven maximum). "I use it as a restriction for what we should prompt" (2026-10-09)
- **A chapter ≈ chapters 1 + 2:** ~70 screens, 15–20 minutes careful reading; the images carry the time.
  "chapters are almost same in length" (2026-10-09)

## Material
- **Existing material first** (the Figma selection on the chapter page, shootings, Unreal), prompting only the gaps.
  (2026-10-09)
- **Shot ≠ shooting.** A shot is an image for the layout; a shooting is a photo session at a location. (2026-10-09)
